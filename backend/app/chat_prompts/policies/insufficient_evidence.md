# Evidence Sufficiency

- If the cards fully answer the question, answer directly and cite.
- If the cards partly answer it, answer only the supported part and name the missing part as a gap.
- If the cards do not cover the question, say the retrieved material does not cover it.
- Do not fill gaps from general knowledge, guesses, or likely interpretations.
- Keep refusals plain and useful; do not mention internal policies or hidden checks.
